Every number below is a macro in `paper/numbers.tex` (generated by `scripts/make_numbers.py` from results files; ledger RUN "paper numbers: numbers.tex generated ..."). **Status labels.** CONFIRMATORY = a preregistered test or estimate, reported as preregistered. EXPLORATORY = not preregistered; must say so in text and captions. **Licensed** = a sentence the prereg's wording rules permit.

**Two measurement facts that scope every claim.**
- All depths here patch the fact prompt's **final-token** residual into the final token of neutral carriers and read the object's first-token probability there (P2 prereg section 1; HR prereg B1; audit). They are depths of answer readout at the prediction position, not of the subject position. Nothing here says where a fact is stored.
- The H-R claims (i, ii) and the P2 claims (iii, iv) use different models, facts, templates and correctness filters (table at the end). They are separate studies, not one dataset.

## (i) Exact within-relation co-occurrence adds almost nothing to the marginal R² of depth
- **Status:** CONFIRMATORY (prereg/HR_revised.md H3, frozen 7725c5b). **Not blind:** the prereg (Part C) discloses that an earlier analysis of the same rows had shown a small effect.
- **Permitted wording:** "Adding within-relation (relation-centred) log co-occurrence (EXACT counts) changed the marginal R² (Nakagawa–Schielzeth, total modelled variance) by point estimates of at most `\hrDRsqMax` (0.0016); the upper 95% bound was below the preregistered 0.02 in all three models (`\hrDRsqHiPyOneFour` 0.0037, `\hrDRsqHiPyTwoEight` 0.0084, `\hrDRsqHiOlmoSeven` 0.0104)." It is not a share of within-relation variance. H3 `\hrHThreeOverall` (PASS).
- **Not permitted:** "co-occurrence does not set / does not affect depth" without the bound. The non-gating survival model finds a small, statistically clear association in the same data: HR per log1p co-occurrence `\hrSurvHrPyOneFour` [`\hrSurvHrLoPyOneFour`, `\hrSurvHrHiPyOneFour`] (0.934 [0.917, 0.952]), `\hrSurvHrPyTwoEight` (0.936), `\hrSurvHrOlmoSeven` (0.966), all p < 1e-6 (`\hrSurvP*`), i.e. slightly *later* resolution with more co-occurrence. Say "small", not "none".
- **Companion (CONFIRMATORY, reported not gating):** co-occurrence predicts correctness: OR per log1p `\hrOrPyOneFour` (1.384), `\hrOrPyTwoEight` (1.433), `\hrOrOlmoSeven` (1.283), CIs above 1.
- **Scope:** 3 models in 2 families (Pythia-1.4B, Pythia-2.8B, OLMo-2-7B); EXACT counts (Pile; OLMo-mix stage 1); 1300 facts, 26 relations, 3 templates; all rows (no correctness filter); half-probability depth; within relation only (between-relation frequency differences are absorbed by the relation term).
- **Also relevant, not a claim:** the controlled-frequency pretraining (S6, `reports/GATE_G2.md`, configs/prereg.yaml g2) was CPU-scaled and learned only 21 facts in all seeds; its FAIL (Spearman −0.130) is uninformative and is not cited as causal evidence.
- **Sources:** `results/HR_gate.json` (run HR-none-f431f35-20261121T081116Z).

## (ii) Relation identity explains little of the variance in depth
- **Status:** CONFIRMATORY (HR prereg H1, a positive hypothesis, FAILED in all 3 models; `\hrHOneOverall`).
- **Permitted wording:** "H1 (a relation share ICC_rel ≥ 0.30 with CI lower bound ≥ 0.15) failed in all three models: the covariate-adjusted relation share was `\hrIccPyOneFour` (0.040), `\hrIccPyTwoEight` (0.013) and `\hrIccOlmoSeven` (0.060), with upper 95% bounds `\hrIccHiPyOneFour` (0.119), `\hrIccHiPyTwoEight` (0.075) and `\hrIccHiOlmoSeven` (0.123) (descriptive; the prereg has no upper-bound criterion)." ICC_rel is an adjusted share (covariates include obj_logprob_final; subject random effect), not "variance explained". A failed positive test is not a confirmed null.
- **Not permitted:** "relation identity does not matter / does not set depth" unqualified. (a) Relation orderings are shared across families at ρ `\hrRhoPyOneFourOlmo` (0.519) and `\hrRhoPyTwoEightOlmo` (0.597), one-sided permutation p `\hrPermPPyOneFourOlmo` (0.0028) and `\hrPermPPyTwoEightOlmo` (0.0011): below the 0.7 H2 threshold, not zero. (b) On correct rows only (non-gating) the point ICC is `\hrIccCorrectOnlyPyOneFour` (0.139), `\hrIccCorrectOnlyPyTwoEight` (0.371), `\hrIccCorrectOnlyOlmoSeven` (0.178). (c) In P2 the relation share of total D1 variance is `\ptShareRelationLlama` (0.081) and `\ptShareRelationOlmo` (0.128).
- **Scope:** as (i). The HR decision under its frozen rule was `\hrDecision` (NO-GO) for the relation-driven-depth programme.
- **Sources:** `results/HR_gate.json`.

## (iii) The fact share is above zero (P2)
- **Status:** CONFIRMATORY. **The only licensed headline sentence (prereg section 6; human GO G2, 2026-11-25): "The fact share is above zero."** `\ptClaimFactShare` (licensed).
- **Evidence:** parametric fact-share null p `\ptNullPLlama` = `\ptNullPOlmo` (0.0010, the floor of `\ptNullNSimLlama` = 1000 null datasets); observed ICC(1) at least `\ptNullRatioMin` (5.6) times the null maximum (`\ptNullMaxLlama` 0.055, `\ptNullMaxOlmo` 0.046).
- **Meaning (GO G2, RESULTS section 4):** fact-level variance, including answer-token identity, is above zero. It does not say that facts have their own depth or that the share is large.
- **Estimates that go with it (intermediate results, prereg section 6; no sentence licensed by them):**
  - fact share ICC(1), D1: `\ptIccDOneLlama` [`\ptIccDOneLoLlama`, `\ptIccDOneHiLlama`] (0.310 [0.242, 0.370]), `\ptIccDOneOlmo` [..] (0.422 [0.347, 0.487]); D4: `\ptIccDFourLlama` (0.282), `\ptIccDFourOlmo` (0.373). Range over models × D1/D4: `\ptIccRangeLo`–`\ptIccRangeHi` (0.28–0.42).
  - wording share (paraphrase main effects + fact × paraphrase interaction), D1: `\ptWordingDOneLlama` (0.649), `\ptWordingDOneOlmo` (0.546); D4: `\ptWordingDFourLlama` (0.678), `\ptWordingDFourOlmo` (0.612).
  - of the wording share, EXPLORATORY split (D1): systematic wording (rt + frame) `\explSysWordShareLlama` (0.177) / `\explSysWordShareOlmo` (0.203); fact × paraphrase `\explFtShareLlama` (0.473) / `\explFtShareOlmo` (0.343). The preregistered "wording shifts together" quantity is negative: `\ptTfResidLlama` (−0.323), `\ptTfResidOlmo` (−0.168).
  - H1 (wording > fact): `\ptHOneLlama` (met) in Llama, `\ptHOneOlmo` (not met) in OLMo (D1 diff CI lower bound `\ptDiffDOneLoOlmo` = −0.004); family-level `\ptClaimHOne` (not licensed). H1-alt not met in either.
- **On the user's arc wording "fact identity explains about 0.3 to 0.4 ..., wording most of the rest":**
  - "0.3 to 0.4" → use `\ptIccRangeLo`–`\ptIccRangeHi` (D1 and D4, two models).
  - "fact identity" → "the fact component, which includes answer-token identity (the frozen formula has no relation × object term; skeptic G2 finding 3)".
  - "wording most of the rest" is not licensed as a finding (H1 fails at family level) and is misleading as a description: most of the non-fact variance is fact-specific sensitivity to paraphrase, not a shift common to all facts. Permitted: "the rest is mostly fact × paraphrase interaction".
- **Scope:** Llama-3.1-8B and OLMo-2-1124-7B only (2 families; `\ptNFamiliesIncluded`); Qwen3-8B, Pythia-1.4B, Pythia-2.8B MISSING (insufficient data: `\ptIncFactsQwen` 125, `\ptIncFactsPyOneFour` 91, `\ptIncFactsPyTwoEight` 93 facts with ≥ 2 correct paraphrases, rule ≥ 150); correct cells only (`\ptNCellsLlama` 1221 and `\ptNCellsOlmo` 1355 of 3000); conditional on four covariates and the carrier effect; within relation; 10 designed frames.
- **Caveats to state with it:** null mildly liberal in calibration (`\ptCalFcOneNcThreeRejOne` = 0.020 at p < 0.01, N = `\ptCalFcOneNcThreeN` 200) and power `\ptCalPowerRejOne` (0.46) at a fact share of 0.10; the margin here is large (observed ≥ 5 × null max). ## (v) Methods: rank-1 causal depth is conditioned on correctness by construction
- **Status:** a derivation plus an EXPLORATORY audit (not preregistered). Label EXPLORATORY in text. Methods note, not a contribution (novelty-scout G4). Not in the abstract.
- **Statement (scoped to the final-token patch; S3 also stored a subject-position measure, causal_subj, for which this derivation does not apply):** with the final-token patch, the patched state at the last block is the source's own final residual, so "rank 1 at every layer from l to the last" requires rank 1 at the last layer, i.e. a correct answer. Rank-1 depth therefore exists only for correct prompts, and every censored prompt is a wrong one.
- **Audit numbers:** Pythia-1.4B, all 3900 rows: resolved & correct `\explAuditResCorrPyOneFour` (720), resolved & wrong `\explAuditResWrongPyOneFour` (0), censored & correct `\explAuditCensCorrPyOneFour` (0), censored & wrong `\explAuditCensWrongPyOneFour` (3180); Pythia-2.8B 804 / 0 / 0 / 3096 (`\explAudit*PyTwoEight`).
- **Consequence used in the paper:** P2's D1 (half of the final probability) is defined on wrong prompts too; P2 restricts to correct cells by design, and the `all` sensitivity (all cells) is reported.
- **Sources:** `results/C1re/C1re-none-3cee850-20261120T141401Z/c1.json`; `reports/AUDIT_causal_depth.md`; `results/AUDITsum/AUDITsum-none-be31f9e-20261120T142742Z/summary.json`.
