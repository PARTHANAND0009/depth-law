Every number below is a macro in `paper/numbers.tex` (generated by `scripts/make_numbers.py` from results files; ledger RUN "paper numbers: numbers.tex generated ..."). The value shown after each macro is what the file holds today; the paper uses the macro, never the digits. Earlier version of this file (G1 EXPLORATORY candidates): git `48d2ec2`.

**Status labels.** CONFIRMATORY = a preregistered test or estimate, reported as preregistered. EXPLORATORY = not preregistered; must say so in text and captions. **Licensed** = a sentence the prereg's wording rules permit.

**Two measurement facts that scope every claim.**
- All depths here patch the fact prompt's **final-token** residual into the final token of neutral carriers and read the object's first-token probability there (P2 prereg section 1; HR prereg B1; audit). They are depths of answer readout at the prediction position, not of the subject position. Nothing here says where a fact is stored.
- The H-R claims (i, ii) and the P2 claims (iii, iv) use different models, facts, templates and correctness filters (table at the end). They are separate studies, not one dataset.

## (i) Exact co-occurrence explains almost none of the within-relation variance in depth
- **Status:** CONFIRMATORY (prereg/HR_revised.md H3, frozen 7725c5b). **Not blind:** the prereg (Part C) discloses that an earlier analysis of the same rows had shown a small effect.
- **Permitted wording:** "Adding within-relation log co-occurrence (EXACT counts) raised marginal R² by at most `\hrDRsqOlmoSeven` (0.0016); the upper 95% bound was below the preregistered 0.02 in all three models (`\hrDRsqHiPyOneFour` 0.0037, `\hrDRsqHiPyTwoEight` 0.0084, `\hrDRsqHiOlmoSeven` 0.0104)." H3 `\hrHThreeOverall` (PASS).
- **Not permitted:** "co-occurrence does not set / does not affect depth" without the bound. The non-gating survival model finds a small, statistically clear association in the same data: HR per log1p co-occurrence `\hrSurvHrPyOneFour` [`\hrSurvHrLoPyOneFour`, `\hrSurvHrHiPyOneFour`] (0.934 [0.917, 0.952]), `\hrSurvHrPyTwoEight` (0.936), `\hrSurvHrOlmoSeven` (0.966), all p < 1e-6 (`\hrSurvP*`), i.e. slightly *later* resolution with more co-occurrence. Say "small", not "none".
- **Companion (CONFIRMATORY, reported not gating):** co-occurrence predicts correctness: OR per log1p `\hrOrPyOneFour` (1.384), `\hrOrPyTwoEight` (1.433), `\hrOrOlmoSeven` (1.283), CIs above 1.
- **Scope:** 3 models in 2 families (Pythia-1.4B, Pythia-2.8B, OLMo-2-7B); EXACT counts (Pile; OLMo-mix stage 1); 1300 facts, 26 relations, 3 templates; all rows (no correctness filter); half-probability depth; within relation only (between-relation frequency differences are absorbed by the relation term).
- **Also relevant, not a claim:** the controlled-frequency pretraining (S6, `reports/GATE_G2.md`, configs/prereg.yaml g2) was CPU-scaled and learned only 21 facts in all seeds; its FAIL (Spearman −0.130) is uninformative and is not cited as causal evidence.
- **Sources:** `results/HR_gate.json` (run HR-none-f431f35-20261121T081116Z).

  - of the wording share, EXPLORATORY split (D1): systematic wording (rt + frame) `\explSysWordShareLlama` (0.177) / `\explSysWordShareOlmo` (0.203); fact × paraphrase `\explFtShareLlama` (0.473) / `\explFtShareOlmo` (0.343). ## (v) Methods: rank-1 causal depth is conditioned on correctness by construction
- **Status:** a derivation plus an EXPLORATORY audit (not preregistered). Label EXPLORATORY in text.
- **Statement:** with the final-token patch, the patched state at the last block is the source's own final residual, so "rank 1 at every layer from l to the last" requires rank 1 at the last layer, i.e. a correct answer. Rank-1 depth therefore exists only for correct prompts, and every censored prompt is a wrong one.
- **Audit numbers:** Pythia-1.4B, all 3900 rows: resolved & correct `\explAuditResCorrPyOneFour` (720), resolved & wrong `\explAuditResWrongPyOneFour` (0), censored & correct `\explAuditCensCorrPyOneFour` (0), censored & wrong `\explAuditCensWrongPyOneFour` (3180); Pythia-2.8B 804 / 0 / 0 / 3096 (`\explAudit*PyTwoEight`).
- **Consequence used in the paper:** P2's D1 (half of the final probability) is defined on wrong prompts too; P2 restricts to correct cells by design, and the `all` sensitivity (all cells) is reported.
- **Sources:** `results/C1re/C1re-none-3cee850-20261120T141401Z/c1.json`; `reports/AUDIT_causal_depth.md`; `results/AUDITsum/AUDITsum-none-be31f9e-20261120T142742Z/summary.json`.

## Positioning (verified characterisations: `reports/gates/PREREG/citation-verifier*.md`, `results/novelty.md`)
| work | what it shows | how this paper differs |
|---|---|---|
| Hase et al. 2023 (2301.04213) | causal-tracing localisation does not reliably predict where editing works | we do not localise for editing; we ask how much of a patching-based depth is a property of the fact vs the prompt, and find the prompt-specific part large (iii) |
| Chen et al. 2024 (2405.14117) | the knowledge-localisation assumption does not hold for all facts; query localisation | complementary: their facts differ in *whether* they localise; we quantify, per fact, how much a depth measurement moves with paraphrase, with a preregistered variance decomposition |
| Gupta et al. 2025 (2510.18871) | the first token of multi-token answers emerges deeper (TunedLens) | we control answer token count as a covariate; our depth is a patched readout, not a lens |
| Hochman, Shapira, Goldberg 2026 (2606.21345) | retrieval is redundant and non-contiguous; per-relation path length shows rank discordance across models | different measure (minimal patched path) and unit (relation); we decompose fact vs wording within relation, and find relation orderings only moderately shared (ii) |
| Elhelo, Globerson, Geva 2026 (2606.27237) | knowledge is encoded task-specifically; different queries about a fact use different parameters | closest: query dependence of a fact. We measure it on a depth readout under paraphrase (same task), estimate its share, and give a D-study |
| Benz et al. 2026 (2607.01000) | KnowledgeDebugger, a tool for exploring localisation and editing | tool paper; no depth or prompt-dependence findings; cite as tooling context only |
Also cited (verified): Agarwal 2609.17537 (relation onset before entity onset; eight prompt families), Merullo et al. 2504.12459 (co-occurrence and linear representations at relation level).
