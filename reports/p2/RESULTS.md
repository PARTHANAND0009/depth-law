Only the `correct` set is gating; all other sets are preregistered sensitivities (not gating), and `h4` is EXPLORATORY.

What is measured (prereg section 1, Measurement): for each fact × paraphrase, the residual at the prompt's **final token** is patched, layer by layer, into the final token of each neutral carrier; D1, D2 and D4 are read from p(object first token) there and D3 from its rank (prereg section 2). The depths are therefore depths of answer readout at the prediction position; this design does not patch the subject position. All depths are defined on correct cells only (set `correct`: the model's top-1 next token on the paraphrase is the object's first token), and only Llama-3.1-8B and OLMo-2-7B are included (section 2), so every family-level claim needs both of them. Limitations: section 8.

## 8. Limitations (restating prereg sections 3, 4 and 10; the figures quoted here are from the frozen prereg and `results/P2null_calib/`, not from this analysis)
- Scope: 2 included families (llama31, olmo2); Qwen3 and both Pythia models are MISSING (insufficient data). Every family-level claim needs both included families; nothing here generalises beyond these models.
- Correct-only scope: depths exist only on cells where the model's top-1 next token is the object's first token. The ≥ 2-correct-paraphrases count is the model inclusion rule (section 2); within an included model, every fact with at least one correct paraphrase enters the `correct` set (so it has more facts than the section 2 count). Selection is on the outcome's support and may be MNAR (prereg section 3); the shares describe facts these models answer, under paraphrases they answer.
- ICC(1) is conditional on the four covariates (log_cooc_proxy, a DCLM phrase count; subject and object token counts; answer prior) and the carrier fixed effect, and it is within relation. It is not comparable with the G1 values.
- The wording share describes variation over the 10 designed frames only (9 df for the frame component).
- The frequency covariate is a PROXY count (DCLM phrase count), not a count from either included model's pretraining corpus.
- CI method: parametric bootMer was selected on simplified simulations; ICC(1) coverage was 0.925 in one condition (prereg section 10).
- MoM is a direction check only; its diff differs from the REML diff by (e − fc)/(nc·den).
- D1 and D4 are both computed from the same patched curve relative to p_final, so agreement between them is weak corroboration, not two independent measurements. The fact share also varies across D1–D4 (section 3), so no cross-model comparison of fact-stability is made.
- The fact component includes answer-token identity: there is no relation × object term in the frozen formula.
- Fact-share null: mildly liberal with large fact × carrier variance (0.020 at p < 0.01 in calibration) and power 0.46 at a fact share of 0.10 (B = 100; prereg section 10).
- P2 does not test frequency. Nothing here says frequency does or does not matter for depth.
- Readout is at the final (prediction) position after patching the final-token residual; the subject position is not patched (S3 also stored a subject-position measure, causal_subj; P2 does not use it).
- The robustness simulation is reported in section 9 (non-selecting).

Future work (not run; would need a new preregistration or amendment; human decision 2026-11-25): the G2 skeptic's follow-up, i.e. the same 300 × 10 design with subject-last-token patching and a depth rule defined for that position, fitted with the frozen formula plus a (1|relation:object) term, on Llama-3.1-8B and OLMo-2-7B. It would separate answer-token identity from the fact component and test whether a subject-position depth is more fact-stable than the final-position readout.
