## What the numbers show (no claims beyond them)
- **A1:** matching the edit layer to the predicted depth leaves s_maha almost unchanged: 0.904 vs 0.911 for rome_single. The causal depth of edited facts barely moved: median 0.727 vs 0.725. In the pilot, the edit layer changed causal depth by less than 0.06 for layers 3-14; only layer 17 moved it, to 0.906.
- **A2:**
  - The logit-lens penalty pushed logit-lens depth later (median 0.986 vs 0.931) and causal depth slightly later (0.769 vs 0.725).
  - It also lowered r_conf (median 3.78 vs 4.24), and s_maha fell from 0.911 to 0.820. A2 costs 1.5x the compute of A1 per edit (30.6 vs 20.0 s), and paraphrase efficacy is 0.60 vs 0.67.
  - The confidence residual alone still scores 0.911.
- **A3:**
  - Six times the synthetic exposure gave a model that the depth/confidence detector catches better than synthdoc50 (s_maha 0.732 vs 0.626), not worse.
  - The EXACT Pile counts the detector uses are unaffected by the synthetic docs. So the extra exposure raises confidence (median r_conf 5.28 vs 5.20) without raising the counts.
  - Confidence-only (0.948) and perplexity (0.951) score highest on A3.
- **Signed depth residual:** below 0.5 on every attack model, as in S8. Edited facts resolve earlier than the clean law predicts, and the attacks did not reverse that.
