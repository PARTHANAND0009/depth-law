(up to corpus composition differences). Main facts, window 100:

Reading (not a measured claim): with `max_clause_freq=500000`, clauses above the cap are subsampled; when both
sides are frequent, in-window matches are rarely sampled, giving 0 or undercounts. The Pile counts are affected
by the same mechanism (83% approx), so no index is a clean reference for the main facts. SANSKRITI facts are
rarer: 91% of Pile counts are non-approx, so its Pile zeros are mostly real zeros.
