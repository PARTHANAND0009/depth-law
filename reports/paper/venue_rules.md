## ARR / ACL 2027 long papers (5 lines)
1. **Length:** up to 8 pages of content for review; unlimited references; Limitations and an optional Ethics section after the conclusion do not count; appendices after the references do not count and reviewers need not read them; +1 page (9) for camera-ready; over-length papers are desk-rejected. [ARR CFP; ACL 2027 call]
2. **Limitations:** a section titled "Limitations" is required, at the end of the paper before the references, not counted toward the limit; it "should not introduce new methods, analysis, or results"; papers without it are desk-rejected. [ARR CFP]
3. **Responsible NLP checklist:** mandatory, filled in a form in the author console (up to 48 h after the deadline per the ARR dates/authors pages); "Yes" answers must cite paper sections; incomplete or misleading answers can be desk-rejected; section E asks about AI assistants in research, coding or writing. [ARR checklist page; ARR authors; ARR dates]
4. **Anonymity and preprints:** two-way anonymized review; since 2024-02-15 there is no anonymity period and non-anonymous preprints are allowed during review, but preprint status must be declared on the form; supplementary material and repository links must be anonymized (Anonymous GitHub given as an example; download-tracking hosts such as Dropbox not allowed); cite own work in the third person; no dual submission. [ARR CFP; ARR authors; ACL anonymity committee report]
5. **Dates and format:** ARR submission deadline Jan 4, 2027, 11:59 PM UTC−12 (AoE); commitment deadline not yet announced; official ACL style files (github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files), unmodified; authors need complete OpenReview profiles (with ORCID) within 1 week of the deadline; a qualified service contributor is needed for guaranteed review (sustainable-reviewing policy, Oct 2026). [ACL 2027 call; ARR CFP]

## ACL policy on AI assistance (5 lines)
1. **Authorship:** "Generative AI tools and technologies may not be listed as authors of a submission"; ACL "does not consider a generative model to be an entity that can fulfill the requirements of co-authorship." [ACL Publication Ethics Policy §4.2.2]
2. **Responsibility:** "Authors are responsible for all content submitted"; ARR: "all authors are fully responsible for the correctness of their methods, results, and writing." [ACL policy; ARR CFP]
3. **What must be disclosed:** use beyond proofreading must be disclosed in the Responsible NLP checklist (item E1) and described in the Acknowledgements; generated text must be marked where used and checked for accuracy and citations; use of models for new ideas must be disclosed. [ARR CFP "AI Writing/Coding Assistance Policy"; ACL policy]
4. **What need not be disclosed:** grammar/spell checkers that only proofread, and short-form predictive typing. [ARR CFP; ACL policy]
5. **For this paper:** experiments, analysis code and drafting were done with Claude Code agents, so disclosure is required in the checklist (E1) and the Acknowledgements (de-anonymizing details removed for review), and the human author must take responsibility for all content. [follows from 1–4]
