Required explicit comparison (measure, models, difference from activation-patching depth on correctly answered facts):
- 2606.21345 (opened HTML; PDF text unreadable): activation locking (write the entity representation at layer l into all higher layers of the same entity) and minimal layer-set paths; Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen3-8B, CounterFact, correctly predicted prompts; alternative paths in 80.1% (Llama) and 82.7% (Qwen). Difference: it locks inside the same prompt and outputs path length or termination layer; depth-law writes the final-token residual into neutral carrier prompts and outputs a normalized layer where patched probability stays >= 50% of final.
- 2308.09124 (abstract opened): LRE, a first-order linear approximation of subject-to-object map, over factual, commonsense and linguistic relations. The abstract names no models. The earlier check lists GPT-J, GPT2-xl and LLaMA-13B with cross-model relation-wise faithfulness correlations 0.85/0.71/0.58; not re-opened, so unverified. Not a patching-depth measure.
